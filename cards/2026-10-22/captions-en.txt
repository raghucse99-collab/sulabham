🗓️ Chandrayaan-1: India's first mission to the Moon
Launched from Sriharikota (AP) 18 years ago — it helped confirm water molecules on the Moon.
⚠️ Exam trap: Chandrayaan-1 was an ORBITER with an impact probe — it did not soft-land. It used PSLV (not GSLV). Jawahar Point (Chandrayaan-1 probe) ≠ Shiv Shakti point (Chandrayaan-3 landing).

📰 Ladakh: Centre's draft for an elected body under a new Article 371(K)
Ladakh groups rejected the MHA's draft 'in principle'; the next round of talks is in New Delhi on 22 October.
⚠️ Exam trap: Article 371(K) does NOT exist yet — it is only a proposal. Ladakh is a UT WITHOUT a legislature (like Chandigarh), while J&K is a UT WITH a legislature (like Delhi, Puducherry).

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 22 Oct 2026

📰 Supreme Court: conversion alone does not give backward class status
SC upheld the Madras HC — a convert must prove membership of a notified backward community, not just the new religion.
⚠️ Exam trap: BC status (Art. 15(4)/16(4)) is possible for backward Muslim groups — but SC status is limited to Hindus, Sikhs and Buddhists by the 1950 Order. Don't mix the two rules.

TBMs, high-altitude shotcrete machines and some dredger parts will need a licence — Indian metro, rail and tunnel projects may be hit.
