📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong: Kakori = train action, 9 Aug 1925; Chauri Chaura = police station burnt, 1922.
2. (a) Only I is incorrect: Azad escaped arrest; he reorganised the HRA in 1928 as the HSRA.
3. (c) I is wrong (PSLV-C11 was used); III is wrong (Jawahar Point = Chandrayaan-1 probe; Chandrayaan-3 = Shiv Shakti point).
4. (d) IV is wrong: contact was lost on 29 Aug 2009, after about 10 months.
5. (a) I and III are incorrect: Ladakh is a UT WITHOUT a legislature; 371(K) is only a proposal.
6. (b) III is wrong: 371G = Mizoram; Sikkim = 371F.
7. (d) III is wrong: a Jamath certificate is only evidence, not final proof.
8. (a) III is wrong: Indra Sawhney accepted that backward groups among non-Hindus can also be Backward Classes.
9. (c) III is wrong (licence, not ban); IV is wrong (India is in MTCR, Wassenaar, Australia Group — not NSG).
10. (a) IV is wrong: MOFCOM and the General Administration of Customs announced it together.
