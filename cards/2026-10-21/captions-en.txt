🗓️ Police Commemoration Day
India remembers police personnel who died on duty — the day goes back to the Hot Springs ambush in Ladakh, 1959.
⚠️ Exam trap: The day remembers the 1959 Hot Springs ambush — not the 1962 war. And the National Police Memorial is in Chanakyapuri, Delhi — not in Ladakh. 'Police' is a State subject, not a Union List subject.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 21 Oct 2026

🗓️ Azad Hind Government (1943)
On 21 October 1943 in Singapore, Subhas Chandra Bose announced a government of 'free India' that led the INA against British rule.
⚠️ Exam trap: Bose did not found the first INA — Mohan Singh did (1942). Azad Hind Government = 1943, Singapore. Don't confuse with the 1915 Provisional Government of India at Kabul (Raja Mahendra Pratap).

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 21 Oct 2026

📰 Reservoirs low after the monsoon — Pennar basin worst hit
India's 178 major reservoirs are 16% below normal; the Pennar basin (AP) has a 72% deficit and Somasila and Srisailam are far below normal.
⚠️ Exam trap: Somasila is on the Penna, not the Krishna. And '68.58%' is storage as a share of capacity — the deficit vs normal is a different number (16.44%). Don't mix the two.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 21 Oct 2026

📰 Supreme Court: internet access is a fundamental right — for children too
To protect children online, target the harmful content and the offenders — not the child. No blanket ban, but safeguards like 'child mode'.
⚠️ Exam trap: The Court did NOT ban social media for children, nor did it say every limit is illegal — it allowed proportionate safeguards. Also, the right comes from Art. 21 read with 19(1)(a) — not from Art. 21A (education).

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 21 Oct 2026

📰 Revamp the functioning of Parliament
Before thinking of more MPs, Parliament must sit longer, protect Question Hour and debate Bills and the Budget properly.
⚠️ Exam trap: The '120 sitting days' is only a recommendation of the NCRWC (2002) — the Constitution fixes no minimum; it only says the gap between sessions can't exceed six months (Art. 85). And the 131st Amendment Bill (850 seats) was negatived — it is not law.
