🗓️ Rani Chennamma of Kittur
On 23 October 1824 her forces defeated the East India Company at Kittur — 33 years before the Revolt of 1857.
⚠️ Exam trap: Kittur (1824) was NOT annexed under Dalhousie's Doctrine of Lapse — Dalhousie came much later (Satara 1848 was the first). Also: the officer killed was Thackeray, not Col. Deacon (Deacon led the second attack).

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 23 Oct 2026

🗓️ International Day of the Snow Leopard
A UN day since 2024 for the 'ghost of the mountains' — India has 718 snow leopards, most of them in Ladakh.
⚠️ Exam trap: Snow leopard is 'Vulnerable', not 'Endangered' or 'Critically Endangered'. Highest number is in Ladakh (477), not Himachal Pradesh (51) or Uttarakhand (124).

📰 AP's rooftop solar push under PM Surya Ghar
AP is fourth in India in rooftop solar under PM Surya Ghar; the CM wants about 9 lakh more homes connected to meet the target.
⚠️ Exam trap: Free power is up to 300 units per MONTH (not per year). Subsidy is capped at 3 kW — a 5 kW system still gets ₹78,000 only. The scheme is run by MNRE, not the Ministry of Power.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 23 Oct 2026

📰 Cancer drugs: 30% trade-margin cap; SC asks for more
From 9 November, 137 anti-cancer drugs will have a 30% trade-margin cap; but only about 16% of overcharging penalties have been recovered.
⚠️ Exam trap: NPPA is under the Department of Pharmaceuticals (Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers), NOT the Health Ministry. The 16% margin is for the ceiling-price formula of scheduled drugs; the new 30% cap is for non-scheduled cancer drugs.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 23 Oct 2026

📰 1% green jet fuel for international flights from January 2027
India is set to make airlines blend 1% Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) on international flights from 2027, as ICAO's CORSIA becomes mandatory.
⚠️ Exam trap: CORSIA belongs to ICAO (civil aviation), not to the UNFCCC or the IMO (shipping). The 1% mandate is first for INTERNATIONAL flights, not domestic ones; the 5% by 2030 figure is still a proposed (indicative) target.
