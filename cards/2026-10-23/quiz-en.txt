🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 23 OCT 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(b) · 6-(d) · 7-(c) · 8-(a) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong: the Doctrine of Lapse was Dalhousie's policy (first used for Satara, 1848) — long after Kittur 1824.
2. (d) III is wrong: Thackeray was killed in October 1824; Col. Deacon led the larger force that captured the fort in December.
3. (a) II is wrong: Ladakh has the most (477); Himachal Pradesh has 51.
4. (c) Only III is incorrect: the snow leopard is 'Vulnerable' (IUCN, 2017), with about 2,710–3,380 mature individuals.
5. (b) II is wrong: the free electricity is up to 300 units per MONTH, not per year. I is right because the subsidy is capped at 3 kW.
6. (d) II is wrong: PM Surya Ghar is run by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE).
7. (c) IV is wrong: the order was issued by the NPPA, an attached office of the Department of Pharmaceuticals (Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers).
8. (a) II is wrong: price rise of non-scheduled drugs is limited to 10% in 12 months. III is right: only ₹1,582.4 crore of ₹10,029.8 crore was recovered.
9. (d) III is wrong: the 1% target is first for international flights. IV is wrong: SAF costs 2–5 times conventional jet fuel.
10. (b) III is wrong: the 2030 figure of 5% is only a reported/proposed target; the official announcement is still awaited.
