🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 30 OCT 2026
1-(a) · 2-(c) · 3-(d) · 4-(c) · 5-(a) · 6-(d) · 7-(a) · 8-(c) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (a) II is wrong: TIFR came first (1945); the Atomic Energy Commission was set up in 1948.
2. (c) III is wrong: Stage 1 uses natural uranium reactors; thorium comes in Stage 3.
3. (d) IV is wrong: the Arya Samaj was founded by Swami Dayananda Saraswati; Raja Ram Mohan Roy founded the Brahmo Samaj (1828).
4. (c) III is the incorrect statement: Dayananda opposed child marriage, along with idol worship and caste by birth.
5. (a) III reverses the shares: the State gives ₹14,000 and the Centre (PM-Kisan) ₹6,000 a year.
6. (d) III is wrong: the 24th instalment was released from Bhildi, Banaskantha (Gujarat). IV is wrong: registered professionals are excluded.
7. (a) III is wrong: the Bench said Forms 6, 7 and 8 are prescribed formats 'cast in stone' that the ECI cannot alter.
8. (c) II is wrong: it was the 61st Amendment Act, 1988 (in force March 1989) that lowered the voting age from 21 to 18.
9. (d) I is wrong: NVS-03 is launched by GSLV-F18, not PSLV.
10. (b) III is wrong: NVS-02 could not reach its intended orbit because its propulsion failed during orbit raising — that is why NVS-03 is important.
