🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 18 OCT 2026
1-(b) · 2-(a) · 3-(c) · 4-(d) · 5-(a) · 6-(b) · 7-(c) · 8-(c) · 9-(b) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is wrong — his Sahitya Akademi Award (1962) was for Viswanatha Madhyakkaralu.
2. (a) Viswanatha 1970, CiNaRe 1988, Ravuri 2012; G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam) got the first Jnanpith in 1965.
3. (c) II is wrong — Lahore (9 March 1846) came one week BEFORE Amritsar (16 March 1846).
4. (d) 1809 Amritsar (Ranjit Singh) → 9 Mar 1846 Lahore → 16 Mar 1846 Amritsar (Gulab Singh) → 1849 Punjab annexed.
5. (a) I is wrong — rolls come under the RPA 1950. III is wrong — AP had a Special Intensive Revision, so it is not in this list.
6. (b) Art 324 = ECI superintendence; Art 326 = adult suffrage; Form 6 = new voter; Form 8 = correction/shifting.
7. (c) IV is wrong — the amendment now ALLOWS private aided/unaided school teachers (and bank, PSU, company staff) to be enumerators.
8. (c) The 1872 Census was NOT synchronous; the first synchronous Census was in 1881 (Lord Ripon).
9. (b) I is wrong — China targets the manufacturing know-how (e.g. perovskite and tandem cells), not solar panel exports.
10. (d) Polysilicon → ingot → wafer → cell → module. India is strongest at the last steps (cells, modules).
