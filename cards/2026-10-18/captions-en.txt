🗓️ Maharaja Gulab Singh
Founder of the princely state of Jammu and Kashmir — through the Treaty of Amritsar, 1846.

📰 Voter list check-up: Special Summary Revision 2027
The Election Commission has ordered an SSR of voter lists in 17 States/UTs with 1 January 2027 as the qualifying date. Andhra Pradesh is not in this list.
⚠️ Exam trap: Voter rolls come under the RPA 1950, NOT the RPA 1951. And SSR (update of the existing list) is different from SIR (fresh, intensive verification).

📰 Census rules changed: who can be an enumerator
The Census (Amendment) Rules, 2026 let States use private school teachers, bank and company staff for Census 2027 duty.
⚠️ Exam trap: First Census = 1872 (Mayo); first SYNCHRONOUS Census = 1881 (Ripon). The rules are under the Census Act, 1948 — the Census Rules themselves are of 1990.
