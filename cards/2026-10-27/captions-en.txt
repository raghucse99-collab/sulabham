📰 Cyclone Arnab: landfall on the AP coast on 28 October
IMD says Arnab will cross the Andhra Pradesh–Yanam coast between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam on the forenoon of 28 October as a very severe cyclonic storm.
⚠️ Exam trap: 'Severe' (89–117 km/h) and 'very severe' (118–167 km/h) are different categories — Arnab is expected as a VERY severe cyclonic storm. The name Arnab came from Bangladesh, not India. Landfall is between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam (close to Yanam), not at Visakhapatnam city.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 27 Oct 2026

📰 FNTA: a new self-governing authority for six Nagaland districts
The President has given assent to the Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority Bill, 2026 — a 62-member body with 46 transferred subjects for eastern Nagaland.
⚠️ Exam trap: FNTA is created by a State law passed by the Nagaland Assembly (with the President's assent) — it is NOT a Sixth Schedule autonomous district council and it does NOT make a new state. Article 371A continues; the region stays outside Panchayati Raj. Article 371A came from the 13th Amendment (1962), not the 13th Schedule.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 27 Oct 2026

📰 India's Ramsar sites reach 103
With Sukhna Wetland (Chandigarh) and Sauj Jheel (Uttar Pradesh) added on 15 October 2026, India now has 103 Wetlands of International Importance — first in Asia.
⚠️ Exam trap: The Convention was adopted on 2 February 1971 but India joined only on 1 February 1982, while India's first two sites are dated 1 October 1981 — do not mix the three dates. Ramsar sites come under the Wetlands Rules 2017 made under the Environment (Protection) Act 1986, not under the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972. Andhra Pradesh has just one site, Kolleru.
