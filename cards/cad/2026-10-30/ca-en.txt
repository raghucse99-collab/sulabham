📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 30 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP Cabinet clears 48 items: quantum hardware park in Amaravati, steel plant in Vizianagaram*
🎯 Exam point: Link to AP's tech push: Quantum Valley (Amaravati) ↔ National Quantum Mission. Trap: the ₹3,700-crore Renaissance unit makes silicon crystals and wafers (solar/electronics), not battery cells.

🏛️ *Cyclone Arnab: AP puts preliminary loss at ₹4,563 crore; crops hit on 3.3 lakh hectares in 16 districts*
🎯 Exam point: Disaster management link: a State asks the Centre for help from the NDRF (National Disaster Response Fund) after an Inter-Ministerial Central Team assesses damage. Trap: Konaseema leads in horticulture damage, but West Godavari leads in total crop area hit.

⚖️ *ECI extends SIR timelines in AP, Telangana and 4 others; AP final electoral roll on 30 November*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: preparation of electoral rolls for Parliament and State Assembly elections is under the ECI (Article 324); one general electoral roll per constituency (Art. 325). Trap: the State Election Commission (Art. 243K) conducts panchayat/municipal polls, not the SIR.

⚖️ *Supreme Court: banks must compensate digital-arrest fraud victims when their due diligence fails*
🎯 Exam point: Link: RBI regulates banks under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949; 'mule account' = an account used to pass on stolen money. Trap: the order of deadlines — notify by 30 Nov, operational by 31 Dec (not the reverse).

📋 *PM releases 24th PM-KISAN instalment from Banaskantha: about ₹18,680 crore to 9.34 crore farmer families*
🎯 Exam point: Central Sector Scheme (100% Central funding) under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare. Trap: ₹4.47 lakh crore is the amount already paid (23 instalments); ₹3.15 lakh crore is the 2026-31 outlay.

💰 *Finance Ministry's monthly review projects 7.35% GDP growth for Q2 of 2026-27*
🎯 Exam point: The Monthly Economic Review comes from the Department of Economic Affairs (Finance Ministry); official GDP estimates are released by NSO under MoSPI. Trap: Indian financial-year Q2 = July–September, not April–June.

🌿 *Glacial lake outburst in Bhutan: CWC issues flood advisory for Alipurduar (WB) and Dhubri (Assam)*
🎯 Exam point: CWC (under the Jal Shakti Ministry) is India's flood-forecasting body. The Sankosh is a tributary of the Brahmaputra and marks part of the Assam–West Bengal boundary. Trap: Alipurduar is in West Bengal, Dhubri is in Assam.

🗓️ *31 October — Rashtriya Ekta Diwas: 151st birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel*
🎯 Exam point: Patel, the first Home Minister and Deputy PM, led the integration of the princely states — hence 'Iron Man of India'. Trap: 31 October is his BIRTH anniversary (he died on 15 December 1950); 31 October is also Indira Gandhi's death anniversary.
