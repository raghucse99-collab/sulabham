📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: the Renaissance unit makes silicon crystals and wafers, not battery cells.
2. (c) III is incorrect: West Godavari (76,752 ha) leads in total crop area. Konaseema leads only in horticulture damage (11,229 ha). I and II are correct.
3. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the SIR of electoral rolls is done by the Election Commission of India (Art. 324). The State Election Commission (Art. 243K) conducts local body polls.
4. (d) I and II are correct. III swaps the dates: notify by 30 November, operational by 31 December.
5. (a) All three are correct. Trap: students often think the hold limit is 60 hours or that only big amounts are flagged — the draft says ₹1,000 and 60 days.
6. (a) I, II, III are correct. IV is the trap: ₹4.47 lakh crore is the amount already paid in 23 instalments; the 2026-31 outlay is ₹3.15 lakh crore.
7. (c) I and II are correct. III reverses the trend: merchandise exports ROSE 23.1% (total exports +11%).
8. (d) I and III are correct. II swaps the States: Alipurduar is in West Bengal, Dhubri is in Assam.
9. (b) C (1875) → A (2014) → B (2018) → D (2026). Trap: the Day (2014) came before the Statue (2018).
10. (b) I and II are correct. III is wrong: 31 October is Patel's BIRTH anniversary; he died on 15 December 1950.
