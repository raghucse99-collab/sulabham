📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 21 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP urban polls: Supreme Court asks SEC to decide on extending the nomination deadline*
🎯 Exam point: Art. 243ZA: the State Election Commission conducts municipal elections (Art. 243K for panchayats). Art. 243ZG limits courts in electoral matters, yet the SC can give directions for free and fair polls. Trap: the SEC is a State body, not part of the ECI.

🏛️ *Police Commemoration Day: CM Naidu calls for AI and drone policing; Amit Shah sets 2029 goal for a drug-free India*
🎯 Exam point: Police and public order are State subjects (List II, Seventh Schedule). 21 October recalls the 10 policemen killed at Hot Springs, Ladakh in 1959. Trap: the National Police Memorial is in New Delhi, not at Hot Springs.

💰 *RBI MPC minutes: all six members voted for the hike; repo rate now 5.50%*
🎯 Exam point: MPC has 6 members (3 from RBI incl. Governor, 3 nominated by the Centre) under the RBI Act, 1934; inflation target 4% ± 2%. Minutes are published on the 14th day. Trap: SDF is the floor and MSF the ceiling of the rate corridor.

⚖️ *CJI Surya Kant opens the Ladakh Bench of the J&K and Ladakh High Court at Leh*
🎯 Exam point: Art. 231 lets Parliament set up one common High Court for two or more States/UTs — J&K and Ladakh share one. Trap: Ladakh got a Bench, not a separate High Court.

🌏 *Polish PM Donald Tusk on a three-day visit to India (21–23 Oct)*
🎯 Exam point: Poland: capital Warsaw, currency złoty, member of the EU and NATO. Trap: India–Poland is a 'Strategic Partnership' (2024), not a 'Comprehensive Strategic Partnership'.

🛡️ *AUSTRAHIND 2026: India–Australia army exercise in Thiruvananthapuram; ₹770-crore anti-drone radar deal with BEL*
🎯 Exam point: India–Australia exercises: AUSTRAHIND (army), AUSINDEX (navy). UN Charter Chapter VII = action against threats to peace. Trap: Garhwal Rifles is India's unit; 13 Brigade is Australia's.

📋 *Shilp Samagam Mela moves to Guwahati; artisans sell under the BHARAT-TULIP brand*
🎯 Exam point: Art. 46 (DPSP): State shall promote the economic interests of SCs, STs and weaker sections. Trap: BHARAT-TULIP of the Social Justice Ministry is different from TULIP, the urban internship programme of the Housing & Urban Affairs Ministry.

🔬 *Kufri Jamunia: a purple potato to fight 'hidden hunger'*
🎯 Exam point: Biofortification = breeding nutrients into crops (e.g. Dhanshakti pearl millet for iron); fortification = adding nutrients during processing (e.g. fortified rice). Trap: Kufri Jamunia is bred conventionally, not a GM crop.

🔬 *Japan launches MMX — the first mission to bring back samples from Mars's moon Phobos*
🎯 Exam point: Mars has two moons — Phobos (larger, closer) and Deimos. Japan's earlier sample-return missions Hayabusa and Hayabusa2 went to asteroids (Itokawa, Ryugu). Trap: MMX samples Phobos, not Mars itself.

🏆 *Vummidi Ethiraj, from the family that crafted the Sengol, passes away at 99*
🎯 Exam point: 'Sengol' (Tamil) stands for just rule, a Chola-era tradition of handing a sceptre to a new ruler. Trap: it is gold-plated silver, not solid gold; it was given by the Thiruvaduthurai Adheenam, not by Mountbatten.
