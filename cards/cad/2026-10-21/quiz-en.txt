🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 21 OCT 2026
1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(b) · 6-(c) · 7-(d) · 8-(b) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is wrong — the SEC is an independent State body; it is not under the ECI. Trap: mixing up the SEC (local bodies) with the ECI (Parliament, Assemblies, President, VP).
2. (c) The National Police Memorial is in New Delhi — Amit Shah spoke there on 21 Oct 2026. Trap: linking the memorial to the place of the 1959 incident.
3. (a) SDF 5.25% (floor) < Repo 5.50% < MSF 5.75% (ceiling). Trap: thinking repo is the lowest rate.
4. (d) All three are correct. Two members wanted to keep a neutral stance, but the hike itself was unanimous. Trap: mixing up the vote on the rate with the vote on the stance.
5. (b) II is wrong — Leh got a Bench of the common High Court of J&K and Ladakh, not a separate High Court. Trap: 'Bench' vs 'High Court'.
6. (c) III is wrong — this is Tusk's second visit as PM; he first came in 2010 during his earlier term. Trap: 'first-ever' statements.
7. (d) AUSTRAHIND = India–Australia army; AUSINDEX = India–Australia navy; INDRA = India–Russia; MMX = Japan's Phobos mission. Trap: swapping AUSTRAHIND and AUSINDEX.
8. (b) BHARAT-TULIP = Traditional Artisans' Upliftment Livelihood Programme of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. Trap: option (a) is TULIP of MoHUA — same word, different scheme.
9. (a) III is wrong — it is biofortified by conventional breeding, not GM. Trap: thinking every 'biofortified' crop is genetically modified.
10. (c) I is wrong — MMX targets Phobos, which is the larger and closer moon; Deimos is the smaller one. Trap: swapping Phobos and Deimos.
