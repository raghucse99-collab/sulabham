📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 25 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Bay of Bengal system to become a very severe cyclonic storm; landfall between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam on 28 Oct*
🎯 Exam point: Yanam is mentioned because it belongs to the Union Territory of Puducherry though it lies in AP's Godavari delta. Trap: landfall is expected in AP (Bapatla–Visakhapatnam), not Tamil Nadu — TN ports only hoisted warning signals.

🏛️ *CM Naidu meets Union Ministers in Delhi: new rail lines, Vande Bharat sleeper Visakhapatnam–Bengaluru, semiconductor support*
🎯 Exam point: Kovvur is in East Godavari (AP) but Bhadrachalam is in Telangana — so this line connects two States. Trap: the agreed sleeper train runs Visakhapatnam–Bengaluru via Tirupati, not Vijayawada–Chennai.

🏛️ *AP municipal polls: 50 of 66 divisions of Kadapa Corporation elected unopposed; polling on 1 November*
🎯 Exam point: Municipalities are in Part IX-A of the Constitution (74th Amendment, 1992); their elections are run by the State Election Commission (Art. 243ZA), not the Election Commission of India. Kadapa is in YSR Kadapa district (Rayalaseema).

💰 *PM inaugurates RBI's second Data Centre at Jatni, Khordha (Odisha)*
🎯 Exam point: RBI runs RTGS and NEFT, but UPI is run by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) — a common trap. RBI was set up in 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934.

🌏 *India and China to discuss better understanding of the LAC in some areas: MEA*
🎯 Exam point: ITBP (under the Home Ministry) guards the India–China border; it was raised on 24 October 1962. In the eastern sector the boundary India accepts is the McMahon Line (Simla Convention, 1914). Trap: LAC ≠ LoC (LoC is with Pakistan).

🛡️ *'Wings Over Ganges': IAF air show over the Ganga at Varanasi to mark its 94th anniversary*
🎯 Exam point: IAF was formed on 8 October 1932 (Air Force Day), so 2026 is its 94th anniversary. Trap: Surya Kiran flies Hawk jets; Sarang is the IAF's helicopter display team.

📋 *Mann Ki Baat 139th episode: organic cotton in Punjab, Heval river revival in Uttarakhand*
🎯 Exam point: Match-the-list material: Nav-Trinjan → Punjab (Malwa); Heval river → Uttarakhand (Tehri Garhwal). Trap: e-Sanjeevani is telemedicine, not a health-insurance scheme.

🏅 *Asian Para Games 2026 end: India 4th with a record 114 medals (30 gold); PM hails athletes*
🎯 Exam point: Order of the top four: China, Japan, Uzbekistan, India. Trap: India was 4th, not 3rd; and total 114 (not 108, which was the Hangzhou record).
