1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(b) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: TN ports only hoisted warning signals; landfall is expected on the AP–Yanam coast between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam.
2. (a) Deep depression (25 Oct) → cyclonic storm (26) → severe cyclonic storm (27) → very severe cyclonic storm (28). Trap: 'severe' comes before 'very severe'.
3. (d) All three are correct as reported after the meeting. Trap: the sleeper train runs via Tirupati to Bengaluru, not to Chennai.
4. (b) (b) is incorrect: Bhadrachalam is in Telangana; Kovvur is in AP. That is why the line connects two States.
5. (c) I and III are correct. II is wrong: municipal polls are held by the State Election Commission (Art. 243ZA, Part IX-A), not the ECI.
6. (a) Only I is correct. II: it is RBI's second data centre (first: Navi Mumbai). III: UPI is operated by NPCI, not RBI.
7. (d) I and II are correct. III is wrong: ITBP is a Central Armed Police Force under the Ministry of Home Affairs.
8. (b) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the IAF was formed on 8 October 1932 — that is why 2026 is the 94th anniversary.
9. (a) Nav-Trinjan is in Punjab's Malwa; the Heval river is in Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand; the air show was at Varanasi, UP. Trap: mixing up the two north Indian States.
10. (c) I and III are correct. II is wrong: India was 4th — after China, Japan and Uzbekistan.
