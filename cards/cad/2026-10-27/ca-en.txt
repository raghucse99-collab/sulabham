📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 27 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Cyclone Arnab becomes 'very severe'; Great Danger Signal No. X hoisted at Kakinada port*
🎯 Exam point: IMD's port warning system has 11 signals: I–II distant, III–IV local cautionary, V–VII danger, VIII–X great danger, XI = communication with IMD has failed. Trap: Signal X is the highest danger signal — XI is not 'more danger', it means communication failure.

🏛️ *Dr NTR University of Health Sciences, Vijayawada, celebrates 40 years (Ruby Jubilee)*
🎯 Exam point: Jubilee names: Silver = 25, Ruby = 40, Golden = 50, Diamond = 60 years. The Governor is the ex-officio Chancellor of AP state universities. Trap: the university began in 1986 as the 'University of Health Sciences'; the NTR name was added only in 1998.

🏛️ *AP named 'Outstanding Tourism State of India' (Award of Merit) at TAAI's 69th Indian Travel Congress*
🎯 Exam point: Award questions ask 'who gave it'. Trap: this award is from TAAI, a travel-industry body — it is NOT the Union Tourism Ministry's National Tourism Award.

⚖️ *Digital news publishers challenge the Data Protection Act in the Supreme Court; no stay, next hearing 17 Nov*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Right to Privacy is a fundamental right under Article 21 (K.S. Puttaswamy, 2017); press freedom flows from Article 19(1)(a). This case is a clash of these two rights. Trap: the DPDP Act was passed in 2023; the Rules are of 2025.

📋 *From 1 November: annual toll pass of ₹1,500/₹3,000 for private vehicles of people living within 20 km of a toll plaza*
🎯 Exam point: Toll on National Highways is fixed by the Centre under the National Highways Act, 1956 and the NH Fee Rules, 2008. Trap: the local pass works only at ONE plaza; the ₹3,075 FASTag Annual Pass (from 15 Aug 2025) works at all NH plazas.

💰 *Motor Vehicles Rules amended: flex-fuel vehicles can now run on up to E100 (almost pure ethanol)*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: National Policy on Biofuels, 2018 promotes ethanol blending. Trap: a 'flex-fuel' vehicle is not automatically E100 — it can use only the blends it is certified for.

🌏 *12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum ends in New Delhi with the 'New Delhi Joint Declaration'*
🎯 Exam point: India holds the BRICS chair in 2026 and hosted the 18th BRICS Summit (12–13 Sep 2026). India first proposed the CCIT at the UN in 1996 — it is still not adopted. Trap: this forum is of parliaments (Speakers), not of heads of government.

🔬 *PM opens ESTIC-2026, the second Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave, at Bharat Mandapam*
🎯 Exam point: Link: the ₹1 lakh crore Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) Fund supports private-sector research; the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) funds university research. Trap: ESTIC-2026 is the 2nd edition, not the first.

🛡️ *Swavlamban 2026: Navy's 5th innovation and indigenisation seminar held in New Delhi*
🎯 Exam point: Link: iDEX (Innovations for Defence Excellence, 2018) funds defence start-ups; Swavlamban is the Navy's own platform. Trap: Swavlamban started in 2022 — 2026 is the 5th edition.
