1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(a) · 6-(c) · 7-(b) · 8-(d) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and III are correct. II is the trap: signal X is the highest danger signal; signal XI means communication with the IMD warning centre has failed.
2. (a) Kakinada (nearest to landfall at Yanam) got GD-X, Visakhapatnam GD-IX, Machilipatnam GD-VIII and Vadarevu LC-III. Trap: Visakhapatnam is the bigger port, but Kakinada got the higher signal because the cyclone crosses near it.
3. (d) (d) is incorrect: it started in 1986 as the 'University of Health Sciences'; NTR's name was added in 1998 (renamed Dr YSR UHS in 2022 and restored in 2024).
4. (b) I and III are correct. II is wrong: the award was given by the Travel Agents Association of India (TAAI), an industry body — not the Union Tourism Ministry.
5. (a) I and III are correct. II is wrong: the Court refused to stay the Act without hearing the Centre; the next hearing is on 17 November 2026.
6. (c) II, III and IV are correct. I is the trap: the local pass works only at the one plaza it is issued for. The ₹3,075 FASTag Annual Pass is the one valid across NH plazas.
7. (b) I and III are correct. II is wrong: fuel above E20 is only for vehicles designed and certified for it; ordinary E20 vehicles cannot use E85 or E100.
8. (d) (d) is incorrect: the forum will meet in 2027 under China's BRICS chairship. The other three are correct.
9. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the theme is 'Creative Minds, Catalyzing Science, Empowering Technologies'.
10. (c) II and III are correct. I is wrong: Swavlamban is the Indian Navy's seminar, run by its Naval Innovation and Indigenisation Organisation (NIIO), not DRDO.
