📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 23 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Low pressure in the Bay of Bengal may become a cyclone by 26 October — North AP and South Odisha coasts on alert*
🎯 Exam point: Know the order: low pressure → depression → deep depression → cyclonic storm. IMD (under the Ministry of Earth Sciences) gives the warnings. October–December is the main cyclone season for the AP coast.

🏛️ *AP elections: urban local body polls on 1 November; final Assembly electoral rolls on 30 October*
🎯 Exam point: Two different bodies: SEC (Art. 243K & 243ZA) runs panchayat and municipal polls; ECI (Art. 324), through the state CEO, prepares rolls for Lok Sabha and Assembly polls. Trap: the CEO is not the head of the SEC.

🏛️ *AP job calendar: process started for over 70% of 10,060 posts — Group-1 (166) and Group-2 (686) included*
🎯 Exam point: Useful for aspirants, and for static GK: APPSC is set up under Art. 315; its members are appointed by the Governor (Art. 316). Trap: they can be removed only by the President (Art. 317).

⚖️ *Home Ministry's first-of-its-kind pilot to build a Civil Defence network — 600 master trainers from 12 States/UTs*
🎯 Exam point: Civil Defence is governed by the Civil Defence Act, 1968 and comes under the Home Ministry (not the Defence Ministry). Trap: Army helps in training, but the scheme is MHA's.

🛡️ *Rajnath Singh releases the Indian Army's Land Warfare Doctrine at the Army Commanders' Conference*
🎯 Exam point: Doctrine = framework, not a law or a weapon. Trap: it is a Land (Army) doctrine — not the Navy's maritime doctrine or a joint doctrine of the three services.

🌏 *India to host the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum in New Delhi on 26–27 October*
🎯 Exam point: BRICS = Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa + newer members (Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE, Indonesia). India holds the BRICS chair in 2026. Trap: 'Samvidhan Sadan' is the OLD Parliament building.

💰 *Duty-free import of 1 million tonnes of raw sugar allowed till 31 December 2026*
🎯 Exam point: Customs duty is levied by the Union (Union List). Trap: TRQ is not a ban or a subsidy — it is a quota at a lower tariff.

🌿 *Satellite-tagged Amur falcons 'Apapang' and 'Alang' return to Manipur's Tamenglong*
🎯 Exam point: WII is in Dehradun and is under the Environment Ministry. Trap: Amur falcons only stop over in North-East India — they breed in China and cross the Arabian Sea to Africa.

🏅 *Asian Para Games 2026: India crosses 100 medals for the second Games in a row*
🎯 Exam point: Tally changes daily — remember host (Aichi-Nagoya, Japan), the 100+ mark and the Hangzhou 2022 record (108). Trap: the tally is as on 23 Oct; the Games end on 24 Oct.
