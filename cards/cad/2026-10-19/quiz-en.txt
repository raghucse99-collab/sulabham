🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 19 OCT 2026
1-(d) · 2-(b) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(b) · 6-(c) · 7-(a) · 8-(d) · 9-(b) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (d) II is wrong — the State Election Commissioner is appointed by the Governor. Trap: mixing the SEC with the Election Commission of India (Art. 324).
2. (b) Notification 19 Oct → scrutiny 24 Oct → withdrawal till 27 Oct → polling 3 Nov. Trap: withdrawal comes after scrutiny, not before.
3. (a) II is wrong — the Court rejected blanket age-based bans as disproportionate and preferred safeguards like parental supervision.
4. (c) The Court said blanket bans are disproportionate and may push children to unregulated platforms; proportionate safeguards are allowed.
5. (b) III is wrong — the amendment makes staff of private companies, banks, PSUs, societies and trusts, and private school teachers, eligible.
6. (c) II is wrong — the waiver is general, not India-specific. The Graham Act (signed 18 Sep 2026) allowed up to 100% tariffs.
7. (a) Mona won gold (250.0, Games record) and Avani silver (249.6). Pramod Bhagat (badminton) and Bhavina Patel (table tennis) were flag bearers. Trap: Avani is the Paralympic champion but took silver here.
8. (d) IV is wrong — Aditya-L1 orbits the L1 point (towards the Sun), which gives a non-stop view of the Sun. Trap: L1 vs L2.
9. (b) Sukhna (Chandigarh) is a man-made lake of 1958 at the Shivalik foothills. The natural Ganga-floodplain wetland is Sauj Jheel in Mainpuri, UP.
10. (c) II is wrong — urban unemployment (6.9%) was higher than rural (4.2%). Female LFPR: rural 38.9% vs urban 25.9%.
