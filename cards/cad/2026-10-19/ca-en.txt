📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 19 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP rural local body polls: MPTC, ZPTC notification issued*
🎯 Exam point: Local body polls are run by the State Election Commission (Art. 243K/243ZA), NOT the Election Commission of India (Art. 324) — a common trap.

⚖️ *Supreme Court: internet access is a fundamental right — children too*
🎯 Exam point: Link: Art. 21 + Art. 19(1)(a); Anuradha Bhasin (2020) = internet & free speech. Trap: the Court did NOT approve a blanket social-media ban for children.

⚖️ *Census (Amendment) Rules, 2026: more people can be enumerators*
🎯 Exam point: Remember the pair: Census Act 1948 + Census Rules 1990. Trap: the change is in the Rules (Rule 3), not an amendment of the Act by Parliament.

🌏 *US suspends Russia-oil tariff threat — relief for India*
🎯 Exam point: Mains link: energy security and strategic autonomy. Trap: it is a general waiver by executive order, not an India-only exemption.

🏅 *Asian Para Games 2026 begin in Japan; Mona Agarwal wins India's first gold*
🎯 Exam point: Ask-able: venue (Aichi-Nagoya, Japan), first Indian gold (Mona Agarwal), flag bearers. Trap: Avani won silver here, not gold.

🔬 *Aditya-L1 and Europe's Proba-3 jointly decode solar eruptions*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Aditya-L1 = India's first solar mission at L1. AP link: Proba-3 was launched from Sriharikota. Trap: VELC is on Aditya-L1, ASPIICS is on Proba-3.

🌿 *India's Ramsar sites reach 103: Sukhna Wetland and Sauj Jheel added*
🎯 Exam point: AP link: Kolleru is AP's only Ramsar site. Trap: Sukhna is man-made (Chandigarh); Sauj Jheel is the natural Ganga-floodplain wetland (UP).

📊 *PLFS September 2026: unemployment steady at 5.0%*
🎯 Exam point: Know the terms: LFPR = share working or seeking work; WPR = share employed; UR = unemployed ÷ labour force. Trap: urban UR is higher, but female LFPR is higher in rural areas.
