🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 26 OCT 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(c) · 4-(a) · 5-(c) · 6-(d) · 7-(b) · 8-(a) · 9-(d) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and II are correct. III is wrong: names come from a list of the 13-country WMO/ESCAP panel; each member suggests names and a name is never reused.
2. (d) I and II are correct. III is wrong: CPCB is a statutory body set up under the Water Act, 1974 — not created by the Constitution.
3. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the week is a tribute to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel; it always includes his birth anniversary, 31 October.
4. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: Article 315 is about Public Service Commissions (UPSC/SPSC). CVC is statutory, not constitutional.
5. (c) All three are correct. Trap: the states were Assam, Mizoram and Manipur — not Nagaland or Arunachal Pradesh.
6. (d) (d) is incorrect: the MANAS anti-drug helpline is 1933. 112 is the all-India emergency number.
7. (b) I and II are correct (approved on 2 Sept 2024). III is wrong: States and UTs create and maintain Farmer IDs under AgriStack.
8. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the Victoria Mode is a pattern of warm and cool water in the North PACIFIC Ocean.
9. (d) All three are correct. Trap: Mission Mausam is under MoES (Ministry of Earth Sciences), not the Environment Ministry (MoEFCC).
10. (c) Bhatnagar Prize 1994 → Breakthrough Prize 2012 → Padma Bhushan 2013 → Oskar Klein Medal 2026. Trap: Breakthrough (2012) came one year before Padma Bhushan (2013).
