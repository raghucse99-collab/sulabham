📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the link asked for is Godavari–Pennar (and a study of Godavari water to the Cauvery), not Mahanadi.
2. (d) All three are correct (51,902 MPTC nominations). Trap: panchayat polls are run by the State Election Commission, not the Election Commission of India.
3. (a) II is wrong — that was the Karnataka High Court's view. The SC said Section 195 CrPC does not cover 125A, so any private person can complain.
4. (c) (c) is incorrect: the SC upheld the quashing of the complaint, because the records rebutted it — it only rejected the High Court's reasoning.
5. (b) II is wrong: wages depend on work measured by engineers in the electronic measurement book and are paid by DBT — attendance alone does not decide them.
6. (d) III is wrong: it is run by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. Trap — Shivraj Singh Chouhan holds both Agriculture and Rural Development.
7. (a) III is wrong: PLFS is conducted by the National Statistics Office under MoSPI (Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation).
8. (c) II is wrong: net FDI SUBTRACTS repatriation and outward FDI from gross inflows ($11.1 bn gross → $3.3 bn net).
9. (a) III is wrong: the places are Varanasi, Prayagraj and Ayodhya — not Mathura.
10. (b) IV is wrong: the epicentre was in a mountainous area about 390 km northeast of Lhasa, not in the city.
