📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 24 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP irrigation review: Polavaram civil works 90.2% done; 36 priority projects to be ready by December 2028*
🎯 Exam point: Polavaram (Indira Sagar) is on the Godavari and is a national project under the AP Reorganisation Act, 2014. Trap: the new link asked for is Godavari–Pennar (not Godavari–Krishna, which Pattiseema already does).

🏛️ *AP rural polls: 57,626 nominations for 9,829 MPTC and 659 ZPTC seats; scrutiny begins*
🎯 Exam point: 73rd Amendment (1992) gives three tiers: Gram Panchayat (village), Mandal Parishad (intermediate — MPTC), Zilla Parishad (district — ZPTC). Trap: these polls are run by the State Election Commission, not the Election Commission of India.

⚖️ *Supreme Court: any voter can file a criminal complaint against a candidate for a false election affidavit (Section 125A, RP Act 1951)*
🎯 Exam point: Links to voters' right to know candidates' background (ADR case, 2002). Trap: the SC did NOT order a trial of the MLA — it upheld the quashing, only changed the reason. Magistrates can still reject frivolous complaints.

📋 *Upgraded NMMS app with AI face check to stop fake attendance in the VB-G RAM G rural jobs scheme*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: attendance alone does not decide wages — wages are based on work measured by engineers (electronic measurement book) and paid by DBT into bank accounts.

📋 *Khet Bachao Abhiyan–Rabi 2026: nationwide campaign for soil health from 22 October to 30 November*
🎯 Exam point: ATMA = Agricultural Technology Management Agency; KVK = Krishi Vigyan Kendra (farm science centre). Trap: the campaign is run by the Agriculture Ministry, not the Rural Development Ministry — though the same Minister heads both.

📊 *Labour survey (PLFS) to change from January 2027: gig/platform workers and unpaid care work to be counted better*
🎯 Exam point: PLFS gives unemployment rate, LFPR (Labour Force Participation Rate) and WPR (Worker Population Ratio). Trap: PLFS is done by NSO under MoSPI — not by the Labour Ministry or NITI Aayog.

💰 *Net FDI of $3.3 billion in August — third month in a row of net inflow (RBI Bulletin)*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: gross FDI is not net FDI — net subtracts repatriation and outward FDI. FDI policy is framed by DPIIT (Commerce Ministry); the data is released by the RBI.

📋 *Kashi Tamil Sangamam 5.0 from 28 November to 12 December on the theme of learning the Thirukkural*
🎯 Exam point: Thirukkural was written by Thiruvalluvar. Tamil was the first language declared classical (2004); Telugu got the status in 2008. Trap: places are Varanasi, Prayagraj and Ayodhya — not Mathura.

🌿 *Magnitude 7.0 earthquake in Tibet's Nagqu region kills at least 16*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the Himalayas and the Tibetan Plateau were formed by the collision of the Indian and Eurasian plates. The Richter/moment magnitude scale is logarithmic — a 7.0 releases about 32 times the energy of a 6.0.
