1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(b) · 6-(a) · 7-(c) · 8-(d) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and II are correct: MHA is nodal for cyclones, the Agriculture Ministry for drought. III is wrong — the drought team goes to Rayalaseema (Sri Sathya Sai, Anantapur, Kurnool, Nandyal); the delta is the cyclone team's area.
2. (c) II and III are correct. I is the trap: municipal elections are held by the State Election Commission (Art. 243ZA read with 243K), not the ECI.
3. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: GSLV's third stage (CUS15) is cryogenic, using liquid hydrogen and liquid oxygen; the solid motor (S139) is the first stage.
4. (d) NavIC–India, BeiDou–China, Galileo–EU, GLONASS–Russia. Trap: Galileo and GLONASS are often swapped. NavIC is regional; the other three are global.
5. (b) I and III are correct (capex ₹6.55 lakh crore = 53.6% of allocation). II is the trap: monthly accounts come from the Controller General of Accounts (CGA), Finance Ministry — the CAG audits.
6. (a) (a) is incorrect: the chairman is Suresh N. Patel, former Central Vigilance Commissioner. An RBI Deputy Governor (Shirish Chandra Murmu) is only a member. (b), (c), (d) are correct.
7. (c) Well-marked low → Depression → Deep depression → Cyclonic storm. The new system near Andaman (31 Oct) was a well-marked low expected to become a depression. Trap: 'deep depression' is still weaker than a cyclonic storm.
8. (d) All three are correct. Cataract (4.71 lakh) was most frequent; by value, angioplasty (PTCA, ~₹649 crore) led. Trap: do not confuse 'most frequent' with 'highest value'.
9. (a) Only I is correct. II is reversed: freshwater (11 million ha) and marine (5.7 million ha) lag far behind land. III is the trap: restoring 30% is Target 2; Target 3 is protecting 30% ('30x30').
10. (c) I and III are correct (first Games: Hamilton, 1930). II is wrong: India hosted the 2010 Games in Delhi.
