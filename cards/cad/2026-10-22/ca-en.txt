📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 22 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP urban polls: State Election Commission halts the process in 100 urban local bodies*
🎯 Exam point: Art. 243ZA: SEC conducts municipal polls; Art. 243K: SEC is appointed by the Governor and can be removed only like a High Court judge. Trap: the Governor cannot remove the SEC on his own.

🛡️ *SIMBEX-2026: India–Singapore naval exercise begins in Visakhapatnam*
🎯 Exam point: SIMBEX = India–Singapore NAVY exercise (bilateral). Trap: SITMEX is the trilateral one with Singapore and Thailand. Eastern Naval Command HQ is in Visakhapatnam.

🔬 *ISRO to launch navigation satellite NVS-03 from Sriharikota on 30 October*
🎯 Exam point: NavIC = India's REGIONAL system (not global like GPS); two services SPS and RS. Trap: NVS-03 goes on GSLV, not PSLV; atomic clock is from SAC Ahmedabad.

💰 *Core industries grow 7.9% in September 2026 — new series has nine industries*
🎯 Exam point: ICI is a monthly lead indicator for IIP, released by DPIIT (Commerce Ministry) — IIP is released by NSO (MoSPI). Trap: it is now NINE industries, not eight.

🌿 *Andaman volcanoes and Chhattisgarh's Sirpur added to India's UNESCO Tentative List*
🎯 Exam point: A site must be on the Tentative List before it can be nominated for World Heritage. Trap: Barren Island is the ACTIVE volcano; Narcondam is dormant and home to the Narcondam hornbill. Baratang mud volcanoes are not magma volcanoes.

📊 *UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2026 'The Point of Return': 1.5°C will be crossed*
🎯 Exam point: Emissions Gap Report = UNEP; World Energy Outlook = IEA; Assessment Reports = IPCC. Trap: the 45% cut is for 2035, not 2030 (2030 = 18%).

🌿 *India hosts the 7th AgriVoltaics World Conference — first in the Global South*
🎯 Exam point: Link: PM-KUSUM (solar pumps and solar plants on farmland). Trap: agrivoltaics is crops + solar on the SAME land, not solar parks on barren land.

🌏 *'Badr' MoU: Qatar, Türkiye, Pakistan and Indonesia sign a defence-industry pact in Doha*
🎯 Exam point: Badr (Doha) = defence INDUSTRY cooperation, 4 countries; Mecca agreement = mutual DEFENCE, Saudi–Pakistan–Türkiye. Trap: Saudi Arabia is not in Badr; Indonesia is.

🏅 *First-ever Commonwealth Kabaddi Championship begins in Ahmedabad on 23 October*
🎯 Exam point: Ahmedabad will also host the 2030 Commonwealth Games. Trap: this is the FIRST Commonwealth Kabaddi Championship — kabaddi is not a Commonwealth Games sport.
