🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 22 OCT 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(b) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(c) · 9-(a) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is wrong — under Art. 243K the SEC can be removed only in the manner and on the grounds of a High Court judge. Trap: 'appointed by the Governor' does not mean 'removed by the Governor'.
2. (d) III is wrong — SIMBEX is bilateral (India–Singapore navies). The trilateral one with Thailand is SITMEX. Trap: mixing up SIMBEX and SITMEX.
3. (a) III is wrong — NVS-03 (2,232 kg) goes on a GSLV Mk II from Sriharikota on 30 Oct. Trap: assuming every ISRO satellite flies on PSLV.
4. (c) (c) is incorrect — iron ore was added, so the new series has NINE industries. Trap: the old name 'eight core industries' is still used in news.
5. (b) Sirpur (Chhattisgarh) – stromatolite town; Barren – active volcano; Baratang – mud volcanoes; Udaigiri Caves (MP) were added on 30 Sept. Trap: swapping Barren (active) with Baratang (mud).
6. (a) III is wrong — the cut needed is 18% by 2030, 45% by 2035 and 69% by 2040. Trap: putting the 2035 figure against 2030.
7. (d) All three are correct. MNRE hosted it at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi (21–23 Oct); earlier editions were in Europe, North America and East Asia. Trap: thinking AgriPV means solar on barren land.
8. (c) II is wrong — Badr is defence-INDUSTRY cooperation (joint production, technology transfer), not a mutual-defence pledge. The Mecca agreement is the mutual-defence one.
9. (a) Ahmedabad (Veer Savarkar Sports Complex). Trap: Birmingham hosted the 2022 Commonwealth Games — not related to kabaddi.
10. (b) Iron ore 18.4% > Electricity 11.4% > Cement 9.8% > Steel 4.9%. Trap: electricity has the biggest WEIGHT (30.93%) but iron ore had the fastest GROWTH.
