📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 29 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Cyclone Arnab: crops on over 80,000 ha damaged; AP Cabinet meets virtually for the first time*
🎯 Exam point: Disaster Management Act, 2005: states use SDRF first; the Centre adds help from the National Disaster Response Fund after an Inter-Ministerial Central Team assesses losses. Trap: NDRF is both a force (Response Force) and a fund (Response Fund).

🏛️ *Annadata Sukhibhava–PM-Kisan: ₹7,000 each to 46.85 lakh farmer families on 30 October*
🎯 Exam point: PM-Kisan (2019) is a Central Sector Scheme: ₹6,000 a year in three instalments of ₹2,000, paid by DBT. Trap: in AP the yearly total is ₹20,000, but the Centre's part is only ₹6,000.

🏛️ *AP SEC moves MPTC, ZPTC polling to 5 November; municipal polls stay on 1 November*
🎯 Exam point: SEC: Article 243K (panchayats) and 243ZA (municipalities), added by the 73rd/74th Amendments; appointed by the Governor, removed only like a High Court judge. Trap: the ECI (Article 324) does not conduct local body polls.

🔬 *GSLV-F18 to launch NavIC satellite NVS-03 from Sriharikota on 30 October*
🎯 Exam point: NavIC (old name IRNSS) is a REGIONAL system, unlike global GPS (USA), GLONASS (Russia), Galileo (EU) and BeiDou (China). Trap: GSLV first puts the satellite in GTO; the satellite's own motors take it to its final orbit.

🩺 *NPPA cuts MRP of 543 anti-cancer medicine brands from 9 November*
🎯 Exam point: Trade margin = gap between the price at which a company sells and the MRP the patient pays. Trap: NPPA is under the Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, not the Health Ministry.

⚖️ *54th PRAGATI meeting: PM reviews projects worth over ₹16,000 crore in 31 States/UTs*
🎯 Exam point: PRAGATI is an ICT-based platform where the PM, Union secretaries and Chief Secretaries review stuck projects and grievances together — a tool of cooperative federalism. Trap: it is a platform, not a statutory body.

⚖️ *Supreme Court to examine AITUC plea against exclusions from 'industry' in the Industrial Relations Code, 2020*
🎯 Exam point: Bangalore Water Supply 'triple test': systematic activity + employer–employee cooperation + goods/services for human wants. Art 19(1)(c) = right to form associations/unions. Trap: it was a 7-judge bench, not 5.

🌏 *India and Mexico sign Terms of Reference to start Preferential Trade Agreement talks*
🎯 Exam point: PTA = duty cuts on a limited list of goods; FTA/CEPA = duties removed on most trade, often services too. Trap: India's PTA with MERCOSUR does not cover Mexico — Mexico is not a MERCOSUR member.

🛡️ *Rajnath Singh at 13th ADMM-Plus in Cebu; reviews 10-year India–US defence framework with Hegseth*
🎯 Exam point: ADMM-Plus is a DEFENCE ministers' forum; do not confuse it with the ASEAN Regional Forum (ARF) or the East Asia Summit. Trap: China and Russia ARE among the 8 'Plus' countries.

💰 *India's automobile industry is now the world's 3rd largest, at ₹22 lakh crore: Gadkari*
🎯 Exam point: Link: PM E-DRIVE, PLI for auto & ACC batteries, National Green Hydrogen Mission (2023). Trap: India overtook JAPAN (not Germany) for 3rd place.
