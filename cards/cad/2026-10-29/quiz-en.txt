🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 29 OCT 2026
1-(b) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(c) · 5-(b) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(a) · 9-(b) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and II are correct (paddy 70,000+ of 80,000+ ha). III is the trap: ₹5 lakh is ex-gratia for each family of the dead, not a per-hectare crop compensation.
2. (a) I and II are correct. III is the trap: ₹14,000 is only the state's yearly share; the total is ₹20,000 (₹14,000 state + ₹6,000 Centre).
3. (d) ULB polling 1 Nov → ULB counting 3 Nov → MPTC/ZPTC polling 5 Nov → counting 7 Nov. Trap: MPTC/ZPTC polling was earlier on 3 Nov; it was moved because ULB counting is that day.
4. (c) I and III are correct. II is wrong: only three satellites (IRNSS-1B, 1I, NVS-01) give services, while standalone positioning needs at least four.
5. (b) (b) is incorrect: NPPA is under the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers — a common trap.
6. (a) I, II and III are correct. IV is the trap: PRAGATI is an ICT-based review platform, not a statutory body.
7. (d) A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4. Trap: Bangalore Water Supply (1978) was a seven-judge bench that gave the 'triple test' for 'industry'.
8. (a) Only I is correct. II: India had a SURPLUS (exports $5.72 bn vs imports $2.1 bn). III: Mexico is not a MERCOSUR member, so a separate PTA is needed.
9. (b) I, II and III are correct. IV is the trap: the 8 'Plus' partners include China and Russia, along with India, USA, Japan, South Korea, Australia and New Zealand.
10. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the stated target is to become No. 1 within the next 5 years, not 10.
