📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 28 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Cyclone Arnab crosses the coast near Yanam; weakens into a 'severe' cyclonic storm*
🎯 Exam point: IMD ladder by wind speed: depression (31–49 km/h) → deep depression (50–61) → cyclonic storm (62–88) → severe (89–117) → very severe (118–166) → extremely severe (167–221) → super cyclone (222+). Trap: Yanam is in Puducherry UT, not an AP district.

🏛️ *Arnab damage: CM writes to Union Home Minister for early central teams; 16 NDRF teams on the ground*
🎯 Exam point: Disaster Management Act, 2005: NDMA (chaired by the PM) and the NDRF force (Section 44). The Union Home Ministry sends an Inter-Ministerial Central Team to assess damage for help from the National Disaster Response Fund. Trap: NDRF = both a force and a fund — read the question carefully.

📋 *Cabinet approves PM Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana — ₹7,395.55 crore for panchayats (2026-27 to 2030-31)*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Part IX (Articles 243–243O) was added by the 73rd Amendment, 1992; 24 April is National Panchayati Raj Day. Trap: PM-VGSY is Centrally Sponsored (States pay a share), not a Central Sector scheme.

💰 *₹10,000-crore Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme to raise capacity almost tenfold*
🎯 Exam point: Know TEU = twenty-foot equivalent unit (a 40-ft box = 2 TEU). Trap: the largest component is operational expenditure assistance (₹8,891 cr), not interest subvention (₹859 cr).

📋 *Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme approved with ₹1,856.67 crore*
🎯 Exam point: Link: Khelo India + 'Make in India'; ANRF was set up under the ANRF Act, 2023. Trap: same day the Cabinet cleared two manufacturing schemes — containers (₹10,000 cr, to 2039-40) and sports goods (₹1,856.67 cr, to 2033-34). Do not mix the numbers.

💰 *DA and DR raised by 3 points to 63% from 1 July 2026*
🎯 Exam point: DA goes to serving employees, DR to pensioners — a common swap trap. DA is revised twice a year (January and July) using the All-India Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers (Labour Bureau). Note: pensioners outnumber employees.

🌿 *CBD COP-17 in Yerevan: India reaffirms the Kunming-Montreal biodiversity framework*
🎯 Exam point: Static: CBD was opened at the Rio Earth Summit, 1992; the Kunming-Montreal framework (COP-15, Dec 2022) has 23 targets for 2030 including '30x30'; the Cali Fund came from COP-16 (Cali, Colombia, 2024). India's law: Biological Diversity Act, 2002. Trap: only 13 of 18 biosphere reserves are in MAB.

💰 *National Startup Awards 6.0 open till 20 November; new award for student innovators*
🎯 Exam point: Startup India was launched on 16 January 2016 — 16 January is National Startup Day. Trap: DPIIT is under the Ministry of Commerce & Industry, not the MSME Ministry.
