🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 20 OCT 2026
1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(b) · 9-(c) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) II is wrong — Deputy Mayors are still chosen by the elected members, by show of hands. Trap: thinking the whole leadership became directly elected.
2. (a) Low-pressure area → depression → deep depression → cyclonic storm (only then it gets a name). Trap: putting 'depression' before the low.
3. (d) All three are correct. AAI earmarked ₹1,532 crore to automate air traffic management. Trap: thinking DGCA (the regulator) runs air traffic control.
4. (b) Dairy (with sugar and edible oils) was kept OUT of India's concessions to protect farmers. Trap: NZ is a big dairy exporter, so students assume dairy was opened.
5. (c) 2026 CHG — Dushanbe; Universal Centre — Tashkent; 2025 CHG — Moscow; 2026 Summit — Bishkek. Trap: mixing the Summit (heads of state) with the CHG (heads of government).
6. (a) III is wrong — at Paris 2024 she won bronze; her gold was at the 2024 World Championships in Kobe (world record 55.06 s). Trap: mixing Paralympics and World Championships.
7. (d) Rubio is US Secretary of State; Sheikh Abdullah is UAE Deputy PM & Foreign Minister; Sergio Gor is the US Ambassador to India. Trap: similar-sounding posts.
8. (b) II is wrong — India's net-zero year is 2070 (announced at COP26). Trap: 2050 is the target of many developed countries.
9. (c) II is wrong — Qdenga needs two doses, three months apart. The pilot began at Kadayanallur (Tenkasi, Tamil Nadu). Trap: assuming one shot is enough.
10. (b) III is wrong — the World Bank (like the IMF) is headquartered in Washington, D.C. Trap: Geneva hosts the WTO and WHO.
