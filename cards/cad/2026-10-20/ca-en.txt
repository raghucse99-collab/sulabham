📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 20 OCT 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP urban polls: voters will elect Mayors directly; Vizianagaram votes on 29 Oct*
🎯 Exam point: Art. 243R (74th Amendment) lets the State law decide how a municipal chairperson is elected — so direct vs indirect election is a State choice. Trap: Deputy Mayor is NOT directly elected.

🌿 *Low pressure in Bay of Bengal: rain alert for AP, cyclone possible by month-end*
🎯 Exam point: Know the order: low-pressure area → depression → deep depression → cyclonic storm. North Indian Ocean cyclones are named from the WMO/ESCAP panel list, issued by RSMC New Delhi (IMD).

💰 *Record 1.5 crore domestic air passengers in a month; Air Traffic Controller Day*
🎯 Exam point: Days: 20 Oct = Air Traffic Controller Day; 7 Dec = International Civil Aviation Day (ICAO). Air traffic control in India is run by the Airports Authority of India (AAI), not DGCA.

🌏 *India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement comes into force*
🎯 Exam point: Likely question: what is excluded (dairy — NZ is a big dairy exporter) and the visa numbers. Don't confuse with the India–UK CETA or the India–EFTA TEPA.

🌏 *25th SCO Heads of Government meeting in Dushanbe: India calls to end the 'ecosystem of terrorism'*
🎯 Exam point: CHG (PM-level) is different from the Heads of State Council (summit, 2026 in Bishkek). India's 'sovereignty and territorial integrity' line is its objection to CPEC through PoK.

🌏 *India–UAE–US foreign ministers meet in Mumbai; Jaishankar–Rubio talks*
🎯 Exam point: Don't mix groupings: I2U2 = India, Israel, UAE, US; IMEC = India–Middle East–Europe Economic Corridor (announced at G20 New Delhi, 2023). This Mumbai meeting was India–UAE–US.

🔬 *100 GW nuclear power by 2047: 'India's programme is for peace', says Jitendra Singh*
🎯 Exam point: Bhabha's three-stage programme: PHWR (natural uranium) → Fast Breeder Reactor (plutonium) → thorium reactors. Trap: India's net-zero year is 2070, not 2050.

📊 *World Bank Group launches its first digital strategy: AI, internet, jobs*
🎯 Exam point: World Bank and IMF were born at Bretton Woods (1944); both are headquartered in Washington, D.C. Trap: Geneva hosts WTO, not the World Bank.

🩺 *India's first dengue vaccination pilot begins in Tamil Nadu*
🎯 Exam point: Qdenga is foreign (Takeda, Japan); DengiAll is the Indian candidate (Panacea Biotec with ICMR). Trap: Qdenga needs two doses, not one.

🏅 *Asian Para Games: Telangana's Deepthi Jeevanji wins 400m gold with a Games record*
🎯 Exam point: Know the class codes: T = track, F = field; T11–13 = visual impairment, T20 = intellectual impairment. Deepthi is from Telangana (Warangal), not AP.
