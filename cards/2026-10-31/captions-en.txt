📰 Cyclone Arnab: ₹4,600 crore loss — who pays for relief, the State or the Centre?
Central teams came to AP on 30 October to assess the damage — the first step towards extra money from the National Disaster Response Fund.
⚠️ Exam trap: The Centre does not pay 100% of the SDRF: it is 75% for general States like AP and 90% for NE/Himalayan States. Extra help comes from the NDRF only after the IMCT visit and HLC approval — and after adjusting 50% of the SDRF opening balance.
