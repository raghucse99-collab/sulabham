🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 31 OCT 2026
1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(b) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(b) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong: Lahore 1929 was presided over by Nehru. Patel presided over Karachi 1931 (Fundamental Rights resolution, Gandhi–Irwin Pact ratified).
2. (c) I is wrong: Ekta Diwas began in 2014; the statue came in 2018. III is wrong: J&K UT has a legislature like Puducherry; Chandigarh (like Ladakh) has none. II is correct.
3. (a) IV is wrong: World Cities Day is fixed on 31 October and closes Urban October; the start of October belongs to World Habitat Day.
4. (b) III is wrong: urban local bodies come from the 74th Amendment (1992, Part IX-A, 12th Schedule); the 73rd is for panchayats.
5. (c) Correct order: memorandum (II) → IMCT visit (III) → HLC approval (I) → NDRF release (IV). The HLC cannot approve before the IMCT assesses.
6. (a) IV is wrong: NDRF assistance is given 'subject to adjustment of 50% of the opening balance' in the State's SDRF (PIB, March 2026).
7. (d) II is wrong: the benchmark fair value was ₹75.99 per share; ₹81 is above it.
8. (b) II is wrong: OFS (like IPO and buyback) is a minority stake sale method — the government keeps control.
9. (a) II is wrong: Lucknow's PM10 (~135) is still more than double 60. III is wrong: the 2025 winner Amravati is in Maharashtra, not AP's Amaravati.
10. (c) III goes against the idea: today's survey already gives only 2.5% to actual PM reduction; the proposal is to give measured reduction far more weight.
