1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(b) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(c) · 9-(a) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong: 'Bharat Mata' was painted by Abanindranath Tagore; her book 'Kali the Mother' influenced it.
2. (d) Only III is incorrect: her flag had a vajra (thunderbolt) on a red background.
3. (a) III is wrong: the week is organised by the Central Vigilance Commission (CVC).
4. (c) II is wrong: the CVC is a statutory body (CVC Act 2003), not a constitutional one.
5. (b) IV is wrong: gram panchayat polls are in three phases — 26 Nov, 29 Nov and 2 Dec.
6. (a) II is wrong: the State Election Commissioner is appointed by the Governor; the ECI has no role in local polls.
7. (d) IV is wrong: on 5 October the Court only issued notice; it refused to suspend the CEC.
8. (c) II is wrong: the 2023 law does not include the Chief Justice of India in the selection committee.
9. (a) IV is wrong: in the PDS each State decides; DFS reaches only about 7.6 crore people in 4 States.
10. (b) III is wrong: men 25.0% vs pregnant women 52.2%. Children 67.1% (highest); women 57.0%.
