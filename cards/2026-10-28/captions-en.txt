🗓️ Sister Nivedita (Margaret Elizabeth Noble)

🗓️ Vigilance Awareness Week 2026: 'Probity for Prosperity'
The Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) runs this anti-corruption week every year in the week of Sardar Patel's birthday (31 October).
⚠️ Exam trap: The CVC is a statutory body (CVC Act 2003), not constitutional. The selection committee has the Home Minister — not the Chief Justice. Theme 2026 = 'Probity for Prosperity'.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 28 Oct 2026

📰 How can a Chief Election Commissioner be removed?
In a joint affidavit to the Supreme Court, the Election Commission said the CEC can be removed only like a Supreme Court judge (Article 324(5)).
⚠️ Exam trap: Only the CEC has 'judge-like' protection; ECs are removed on the CEC's recommendation. The 2023 selection committee has NO Chief Justice of India — it is PM + LoP + a Cabinet Minister.

📰 Double Fortified Salt: a pinch against anaemia
Salt with both iodine and iron — developed by ICMR-NIN, Hyderabad — is cheap and proven, yet reaches only about 7.6 crore people. The Hindu wants it in the PDS.
⚠️ Exam trap: DFS = iodine + IRON (not folic acid, not zinc). It was developed by NIN Hyderabad. In NFHS-5, anaemia is highest in children 6–59 months (67.1%), not in pregnant women (52.2%).
