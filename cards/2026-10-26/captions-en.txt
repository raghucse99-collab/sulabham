📰 AP's ₹27,370-crore green power grid cleared under GEC-III
The Centre has cleared APTRANSCO's plan to carry solar and wind power from Rayalaseema and Prakasam to users — AP's share of the ₹1.86-lakh-crore Green Energy Corridor Phase-III.
⚠️ Exam trap: GEC-III is for INTRA-state lines (inside a state), built by state utilities like APTRANSCO. 135 GW is renewable CAPACITY to be carried; 50 GWh is battery ENERGY storage — don't mix GW and GWh. Total ₹1.86 lakh crore ≠ central support ₹54,082 crore.

📰 Siang Upper Multipurpose Project: why Arunachal is protesting
Protests against the proposed 11,200-MW project turned violent at Pasighat on 23 Oct; the government sees it as a strategic answer to China's 60,000-MW Medog project upstream.
⚠️ Exam trap: SUMP is only at the pre-feasibility stage — it is NOT approved. Medog (China) ≈ 60,000 MW; SUMP (India) = 11,200 MW — don't swap. Tsangpo = Tibet, Siang = Arunachal, Brahmaputra = Assam.
