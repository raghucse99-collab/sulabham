1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(b) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(c) · 9-(a) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is wrong: Mountbatten accepted it on 27 October 1947, the day Indian troops were airlifted into Kashmir.
2. (d) Only II is incorrect: Ladakh UT has no legislature; only the J&K UT has one.
3. (a) IV is wrong: he was killed on 25 March 1931 in the Kanpur riots, two days after Bhagat Singh's hanging.
4. (c) III is wrong: he stayed in Kanpur during the riots and missed the Karachi session.
5. (b) III is wrong: GEC-III funds intra-state lines; AP's part is built by the state utility APTRANSCO.
6. (a) II is wrong: battery storage is 50 GWh (energy), not 50 GW; 135 GW is the renewable capacity to be evacuated.
7. (d) II is wrong: SUMP is 11,200 MW while China's Medog is about 60,000 MW.
8. (c) Only III is incorrect: the protesters fear displacement and loss of ancestral land, forests, livelihoods and seismic risk.
9. (a) III is wrong: Saudi Arabia and Ethiopia declined to participate.
10. (b) I is wrong: 2026 is India's fourth chairship — after 2012, 2016 and 2021.
