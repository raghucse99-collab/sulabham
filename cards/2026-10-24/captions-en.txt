🗓️ United Nations Day
On 24 October 1945 the UN Charter came into force — the United Nations was officially born.
⚠️ Exam trap: 26 June 1945 = Charter SIGNED; 24 October 1945 = Charter in FORCE (UN Day). India ratified on 30 October 1945 — after UN Day — yet it is a founding member.

🗓️ World Polio Day
India has had no wild polio case since January 2011 — but the virus still survives in Afghanistan and Pakistan.
⚠️ Exam trap: Last case 2011, certificate 2014 — the gap is the 3-year rule. WHO certified the whole South-East Asia REGION, not India alone. World Polio Day is Rotary's day for Jonas Salk.

📰 A cyclone is brewing in the Bay of Bengal — North AP coast on alert
IMD says a low-pressure area will become a depression today, a cyclonic storm by 26 Oct, and reach near the North AP – South Odisha coast around 28 Oct.
⚠️ Exam trap: Depression (31–49) and deep depression (50–61) are NOT cyclones — a system becomes a 'cyclonic storm' only at 62 km/h. Cyclone names are never repeated, and the list is not rotated every few years like the Atlantic list.

📰 Drought in 388 AP mandals: how a State declares drought
AP has declared kharif drought in 388 of 688 mandals in 21 districts; the deadline for other States' kharif declarations is 31 October.
⚠️ Exam trap: Drought is declared by the STATE government, using the Centre's 2016 Manual — not by IMD or the Union Ministry. Rainfall alone is not enough: impact indicators and ground truthing are also needed. AP covered 21 districts, not all 28.

📰 SIM-binding for WhatsApp, Telegram & co. from 1 January 2027
Messaging apps must stay linked to the active SIM of the registered number — if the SIM is removed, the app stops working. Aim: cut cyber fraud.
⚠️ Exam trap: The order comes from DoT (Ministry of Communications) under the Telecom Cyber Security Rules, 2024 — not from MeitY under the IT Act. The fixed six-hour web logout was replaced by a risk-based logout. Start date is 1 January 2027, not 1 January 2026.
