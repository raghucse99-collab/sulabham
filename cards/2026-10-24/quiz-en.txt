1-(b) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(b) · 8-(c) · 9-(d) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) IV is wrong: four Left parties nominated her in 2002 and she lost to A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.
2. (a) Only III is incorrect: she joined the CPI(M) in 1972, but AIDWA was founded in 1981 — she was one of its founding members.
3. (d) I is wrong: the Charter was signed on 26 June 1945; UN Day (24 October) marks its entry into force. India ratified on 30 October 1945.
4. (b) II is wrong: WHO certified the entire South-East Asia Region, including India, on 27 March 2014.
5. (c) I is wrong: World Polio Day was started by Rotary International, to mark the birth of Jonas Salk.
6. (a) II is wrong: 55 km/h is a deep depression; a cyclonic storm starts only at 62 km/h.
7. (b) II is wrong: cyclone names in this region are never reused — once used, a name is retired.
8. (c) IV is wrong: drought was declared in 388 mandals across 21 of 28 districts; Nellore was not included.
9. (d) III is wrong: impact indicators (sowing, vegetation, soil moisture, water) and ground truthing are also needed. IV is wrong: early declaration is allowed from August.
10. (c) III is wrong: the six-hour forced logout has been replaced by a risk-based logout — users are logged out only when needed.
