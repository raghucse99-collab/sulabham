📰 3D-printed homes for the rural poor: AP's pilot under PMAY-G 2.0
177 houses are being 'printed' in Guntur district — described as India's first rural housing pilot using 3D concrete printing.
⚠️ Exam trap: 3DCP prints only the WALLS — foundation, roof, doors and plumbing are conventional (hybrid). PMAY-G is under Rural Development, not Housing & Urban Affairs. The AP pilot is in Guntur district, not Visakhapatnam.

📰 PM Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana: ₹7,395.55 crore to strengthen panchayats
A new Centrally Sponsored Scheme (2026-27 to 2030-31) to put the Gram Panchayat at the centre of local planning, audit and digital governance.
