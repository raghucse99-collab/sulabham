🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 29 OCT 2026
1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(c) · 6-(b) · 7-(a) · 8-(c) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong: Sarojini Naidu led the Dharasana raid. Kamaladevi was in the Bombay salt-making team.
2. (c) III is incorrect: she contested in 1926 but lost by 55 votes; she was never a Madras legislator or Speaker.
3. (a) III is wrong: Maulana Mehmud Hasan laid the foundation stone. Sir Syed (died 1898) founded the MAO College, which became AMU.
4. (d) Karol Bagh 1925 → Okhla 1935 → deemed 1962 → central university 1988. Trap: deemed (1962) comes before central (1988).
5. (c) II is wrong: the pilot is under PMAY-Gramin (rural), run by the Ministry of Rural Development.
6. (b) IV is wrong: building codes for printed walls and regulatory approval are among the main challenges of 3DCP.
7. (a) III is wrong: ₹4.35 lakh crore is the 16th Finance Commission's grant for rural local bodies, not the scheme outlay. IV is wrong: no permanent posts — only need-based contract staff.
8. (c) III is wrong: the Eleventh Schedule has 29 subjects for panchayats. 18 subjects are in the Twelfth Schedule (municipalities).
9. (d) II is wrong: COP-17 is the Conference of the Parties to the Convention on Biological Diversity, not the UNFCCC climate COP.
10. (b) III is wrong: the Aichi Targets were 20 targets for 2011–2020. The 23 targets for 2030 belong to the Kunming-Montreal framework.
