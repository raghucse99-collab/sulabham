1-(b) · 2-(a) · 3-(c) · 4-(d) · 5-(a) · 6-(c) · 7-(b) · 8-(d) · 9-(c) · 10-(a)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong — Andhra State was formed on 1 October 1953. 1 November (1956) is Andhra Pradesh — a common trap.
2. (a) 19 Oct 1952 fast → 19 Dec 1952 Nehru's announcement → 1 Oct 1953 Andhra State → 1 Nov 1956 Andhra Pradesh. The announcement came only after his death.
3. (c) Raman won in 1930; Chandrasekhar gave the 1.44 limit; Fowler shared the 1983 Nobel; Eddington opposed the limit. Trap: Eddington did NOT share the prize.
4. (d) (d) is incorrect — Chandra is NASA's observatory, launched by Space Shuttle Columbia on 23 July 1999.
5. (a) III is wrong — local body polls are conducted by the State Election Commission (Art. 243ZA), headed by Anil Chandra Punetha in AP.
6. (c) 243K = SEC (panchayats), 243U = 5-year term, 243ZA = SEC (municipalities), 243T = reservation incl. one-third for women. Trap: mixing 243K and 243ZA.
7. (b) II is wrong — Defence Minister Rajnath Singh represents India this time; EAM Jaishankar went to the 24th CHG in Moscow.
8. (d) (d) is incorrect — India became an observer in 2005 and a full member in 2017. Trap: observer year vs member year.
9. (c) III is wrong — it was launched on 14 Oct 2024, delivered on 27 Aug 2026 and commissioned on 16 Oct 2026.
10. (a) Contract (Mar 2022) → launch (Oct 2024) → delivery (Aug 2026) → commissioning (16 Oct 2026). Trap: delivery comes before commissioning.
