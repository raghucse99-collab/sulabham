1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(c) · 6-(d) · 7-(b) · 8-(a) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is wrong: the voting age was 21 in 1951–52; it was lowered to 18 only in 1989.
2. (c) III is NOT correct: Ambedkar lost Bombay (North Central) to N. S. Kajrolkar and later entered Parliament through the Rajya Sabha.
3. (a) II is wrong: Picasso co-founded Cubism (1909) with Georges Braque, not Matisse.
4. (d) Blue Period 1901 → Les Demoiselles d'Avignon 1907 → Guernica 1937 → Dove 1949. Trap: Guernica (1937) comes before the Dove (1949).
5. (c) I is wrong: panchayat polls are conducted by the State Election Commission (Art. 243K), not the ECI.
6. (d) II is wrong: States with a population below 20 lakh need not have the intermediate (mandal/block) tier.
7. (b) I is wrong: Section 125A was inserted in 2002 (Act 72 of 2002), not in 1951.
8. (a) II is wrong: the Court said the omission is deliberate (Section 195 was amended in 2006, after 125A). IV is wrong: the complaint stayed closed as the address claim was prima facie rebutted.
9. (d) III is wrong: unpaid-work questions will be asked to all persons aged 15 and above, in both rural and urban areas.
10. (b) II is wrong: 2.35 crore is NITI Aayog's projection for 2029–30, not an actual count.
