🗓️ India's first general election begins (1951–52)
75 years ago today, the first votes of free India were cast in snow-bound Chini tehsil (Kinnaur), Himachal Pradesh.

📰 AP Parishad polls on 3 November: MPTC and ZPTC explained
About 2.81 crore rural voters will elect members of Mandal and Zilla Parishads — the middle and top tiers of Panchayat Raj.

📰 SC: any voter can file a case over a false election affidavit
A private complaint can set Section 125A of the RP Act, 1951 in motion — it is not only for the Election Commission.
⚠️ Exam trap: The Court did NOT punish the MLA — it kept this complaint closed on other grounds. Section 125A was added in 2002, not in 1951. It is an offence under the RP Act, not the IPC.
